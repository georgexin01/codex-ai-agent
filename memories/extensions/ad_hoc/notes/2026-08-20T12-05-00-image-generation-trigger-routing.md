# Image-generation trigger routing extended

- Image-generation routing must activate for exact aliases and for semantic intent: a generation verb such as `generate`, `create`, `make`, `render`, `produce`, or `design` combined with an image noun such as `image`, `photo`, `banner`, `logo`, `hero`, `card`, `profile`, `gallery`, `ad`, or `asset`.
- The route is `codex-router/IMAGE_GENERATION_ASSET_POLICY.md`, followed by `skills/.system/imagegen/SKILL.md`.
- The wording does not need to match one saved phrase, but ordinary image debugging or layout questions must not be treated as generation without generation intent.
