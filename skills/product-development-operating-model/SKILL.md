# Product Development Operating Model

Use this skill when the request needs product-level reasoning across customer value, architecture, routes, UX, metadata, SEO, AI research, or prioritization.

PULSE routes common aliases such as `meta update`, `meta seo`, `news information generate`, `ai news generate`, `ai news articles generate`, `ai search result`, `ai research`, `ai google research`, `google research`, `bing research`, `gemini research`, `chatgpt search`, `search result optimization`, `local seo research`, `geo research`, `keyword catch`, `content gap research`, and `search visibility audit` here. Keep the model dormant for routine edits unless the request has product, search, content, or strategy scope.

## Activation contract

1. Keep explicit user scope, current project evidence, and safety rules first.
2. Separate the verified current 60% foundation from the later 40% advanced backlog.
3. Identify the customer outcome and the smallest end-to-end value loop.
4. Surface missing evidence as `INSUFFICIENT DATA`; do not convert guesses into product facts.
5. Map affected pages, routes, data ownership, integrations, UX states, and verification targets only when the task needs that depth.
6. Finish with the smallest actionable recommendation and state what remains deferred.

## Context-fit and evolution gate

When the task writes or upgrades Codex knowledge, skills, rules, metadata guidance, or project notes:

1. Inspect related current files and triggers before adding new wording.
2. Identify the canonical owner and check semantic duplication, conflicts, scope, terminology, activation breadth, and verification cost.
3. Classify the candidate as `adopt`, `adapt`, `defer`, or `reject`.
4. Put the smallest useful rule in the lowest effective layer: hot context for activation only, knowledge for principles, skills for task behavior, and references for detailed conditional material.
5. Prefer additive, reversible improvements. Do not add a mandatory strategy pass to routine work.
6. Verify reachability, frontmatter, links, trigger specificity, English-only requirements, and the nearest available validator.

Do not promote a one-off observation to an always-on rule unless it is repeatable, reusable beyond one project, low-risk, and clearly better than existing guidance.

## Non-interference boundary

Do not load or apply the full model for a narrow bug fix, mechanical rename, formatting edit, or isolated implementation task unless the user asks for product-level review. Do not expand scope, invent a business identity, generate unsupported SEO claims, or replace technical verification with strategic commentary.

## Metadata and AI research boundary

For metadata, SEO, or AI-assisted research, distinguish verified facts, hypotheses, drafts, and decisions. Prefer useful pages tied to real customer questions and actions. Require human or live-source verification for volatile, legal, financial, safety, inventory, testimonial, and location claims.

## Search visibility and AI-content workflow

When the request concerns Google exposure, AI search, metadata, keyword research, GEO, or article generation, read the canonical note's search-visibility sections and apply this compact contract:

1. Confirm the public origin, crawl access, indexability, canonical URL, language variants, sitemap, robots output, and rendered text.
2. Define one audience, one question, one primary intent, one canonical URL, and one customer action.
3. Gather first-party and authoritative sources; record dates, unknowns, and evidence ownership.
4. Use AI to cluster queries, find gaps, outline, draft, compare, link, and QA. Never let it invent prices, inventory, legal claims, reviews, locations, or results.
5. Check semantic overlap and cannibalization with existing pages and choose `new`, `update`, `merge`, `split`, `defer`, or `retire` before publishing.
6. Ensure title, H1, visible answer, metadata, structured data, internal links, language, and CTA agree.
7. Human-approve volatile or trust-sensitive claims, then publish through the project's SSG/SSR and sitemap path.
8. Measure both Search Console discovery and product conversion; refresh, consolidate, or retire based on evidence.

For local-market work, bind each query and page to a verified service area, audience, language, entity fact, evidence owner, related product or inventory, and primary CTA. Keep `crawl`, `index`, `citation`, `referral visit`, and `lead` as separate outcomes: Bing AI Performance reports supported Microsoft surfaces, ChatGPT requires `OAI-SearchBot` and origin/CDN checks, server logs show crawlers, and GA4 shows tagged or referred visits. IndexNow is a freshness notification, not an indexing or citation guarantee. Treat `llms.txt` as optional and experimental, never as a replacement for visible HTML, robots, sitemap, canonical, or source-backed content.

When a daily publishing number is requested, convert it into a research queue and evidence-gated candidates. Do not let a target such as `+10` or `1,000 pages` override distinct intent, real local facts, product linkage, review capacity, or maintenance ownership.

For ChatGPT Search specifically, allow `OAI-SearchBot` where public discovery is intended and verify the hosting/CDN path as well as robots.txt. Inclusion or citation is never guaranteed.

## Article output contract

When asked to generate an article, return or store:

`intent brief | source set | verified facts | unknowns | outline | article draft | title/meta variants | internal links | structured-data suggestion | CTA | reviewer | refresh trigger | QA status`

The article should answer early, show evidence and dates, explain limits, use a real author or reviewer where appropriate, connect to a useful product action, and disclose meaningful automation when readers would reasonably ask how it was created.

## Publishing cadence boundary

If a daily content target is requested, interpret `+10` as up to ten research signals or candidate questions, not ten automatic public articles. Default to 1-2 evidence-gated pages or substantial updates per week, plus one trust or conversion improvement. If a candidate lacks evidence, distinct intent, owner, or review capacity, defer it or improve an existing page.

## Search-quality boundary

Do not equate publication volume, keyword density, structured data, crawler access, or one AI citation with success. They improve eligibility or learning only. The success signal is a useful page that earns qualified discovery and helps a user complete the next real action.
